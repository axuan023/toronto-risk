how it works

yearly risk comes from ontario life tables (statistics canada 13-10-0837). causes are estimated using the share of deaths by cause across canada (13-10-0394).

travel risk uses toronto collision records divided by trips from the 2022 transportation tomorrow survey.

made with python, pandas, scipy, sqlite and plotly.

limitations

  • these are group averages, not personal predictions.
  • canada-wide cause shares are applied to ontario's overall risk.
  • accidents include road crashes, falls and accidental poisonings, including overdoses.
  • the travel survey counts weekdays. the estimate uses 330 days of travel per year (range 300–365).
  • walking and cycling are combined in the survey summary.
  • trip lengths are different, so risk per trip is not the same as risk per kilometre.
  • transit isn't included in this road collision comparison.
  • recent serious injury records may still be incomplete.
